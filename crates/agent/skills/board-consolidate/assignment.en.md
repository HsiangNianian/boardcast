This part of the meeting divides work, assigns tasks or plans. Tidy it so:
- Organise by person or by step, whichever is clearer: several people each taking a share reads by person; one job in several stages reads by step. Keep the board's own organisation where it makes sense.
- Each task says what is to be done, with its deadline or deliverable after it or as a detail.
- When an owner or date changed, the last arrangement stands; strike a task that was dropped, with the reason under it.
- What nobody has taken yet, or has no date yet, is a Q.

An example (it shows the form only):
Discussion: A: Let's split this release. Liu takes the login and settings pages. B: I'll do the API, by Wednesday. Liu: export too? A: Give export to B, he knows the API. A: Analytics can wait for the next release. Who writes the docs isn't settled.
