01 / listen

it listens.

Press space to start. Speech is recognized on your computer, the transcript fills line by line, and the meter shows it can hear you.

02 / draw

then it writes.

Finish a sentence and the board writes it out, stroke by stroke: a title, items, an arrow. No template, no hands.

03 / correct

fix it in one click.

Click anything on the board to strike, circle, star, rewrite or remove it. All of it can be undone.

04 / circle

circle, then say.

Lasso “canary”, then say “star this”. The model knows what “this” means.

05 / replay

watch it again.

After the meeting, drag the replay bar and the board goes back to how it was. Fork a new board from any moment.
