# ASR candidate baseline · 2026-11-02

[Eval run 41382740700](https://github.com/HsiangNianian/boardcast/actions/runs/41382740700) completed all **26 ASR jobs and the language comparison**: 19 model configurations with VAD on, plus seven preprocessing/VAD/dynamic controls. This statement covers the ASR jobs, not the workflow's separate desktop and board-agent jobs. Raw transcripts, segment timings and checks are in its `asr-*` artifacts; `asr-comparison` contains the complete comparison.

This is the **quick** corpus: 2 Chinese clips / 124 characters with provisional references, 3 Japanese read-speech clips / 162 characters, 3 English meeting turns / 103 words, 8 mixed-language probes / 53 units, plus silence/noise controls. These counts are too small to establish a general model ranking. The full corpus is used by the weekly sweep or by selecting both `asr_sweep` and `full_asr` in Eval.

Parakeet Unified English made 1 error versus SenseVoice's 5 in 103 words; ReazonSpeech made 12 versus 18 in 162 Japanese characters. Paraformer made 14 versus 16 errors on the mixed probes. These are candidates for a larger holdout, not sufficient evidence for automatic language routing. The Chinese reference needs human checking before it can support a model choice. Cohere receives an explicit language; multilingual auto-language rows do not.

The community Qwen 1.7B export needs investigation: its first Chinese clip begins with `language Chinese lassicly` and omits most of the speech. This result describes the pinned export/runtime combination, not upstream Qwen 1.7B quality. Do not promote it or conclude that the upstream smaller model is inherently better from this run.

Native rows reuse desktop segmentation, soft breaks and voiceprints with two CPU threads. CTC reuses desktop VAD intervals and records recognition-only timing; native timing covers the desktop pipeline. GitHub runners are separate machines, and neither timing is microphone-to-screen latency. Consult the artifacts for timings instead of treating these different scopes as one speed ranking.

## Controls and boundaries

All 19 VAD-on candidate configurations passed the speech/silence diagnostics. All four VAD-off SenseVoice/Whisper-base controls produced text on silence, including the GTCRN variants. Those experimental diagnostics are reported without failing their jobs; a green job means the evaluation completed, not that its model is suitable for production.

Dynamic SenseVoice ran 11 second passes, accepted 0 revisions and spent 2375 ms on retry inference. Original-sample accuracy was unchanged, and the paired reference checks passed. Production remains **SenseVoice + dynamic review + VAD on**; there is no automatic model router in this change.

Apple SpeechTranscriber, Nemotron streaming, Voxtral Realtime and alternate Core ML/MLX/GGUF runtimes were not measured. See the [survey and runtime boundaries](candidates.md). Japanese meeting audio, representative mixed-language meetings and larger human-checked holdouts remain necessary before selecting production routes.

## Reproduction

Run `pnpm eval:asr:sweep` for the 19 quick candidate configurations, or dispatch Eval with `asr_sweep=true` to include the seven additional controls. Models and fixtures are downloaded and checksum-verified automatically; the three CTC controls also require `uv`. `--suite full` selects the larger corpus locally.
